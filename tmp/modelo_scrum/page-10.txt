Abstract 
This research aligned with the ninth Sustainable Development Goal (SDG), which 
promotes industry, innovation, and infrastructure. The purpose of the study was to 
determine how a system based on Business Intelligence and Machine Learning 
influences supply c hain management in the company INTERSEGUR. This was an 
applied research study with a quantitative approach and a pre -experimental design. 
The sample consisted of 120 orders, obtained through the simple random sampling 
formula. For data collection, a registration form was used as the instrument. The main 
findings showed that the solution based on Business Intelligence and Machine 
Learning improved inventory accuracy in INTERSEGUR, increasing from 39.44% in 
the pre-test to 49.02% in the post-test. Furthermore, the order fulfillment rate improved 
from 79.85% in the pre -test to 85.88% in the post -test. Therefore, it was concluded 
that the implemented solution had a significant and positive impact on improving 
supply chain management at INTERSEGUR. 
Keywords: Data analysis, Artificial intelligence, Information management, Supply and 
demand.
