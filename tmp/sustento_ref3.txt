ABSTRACT
Modern retail business manages products from various sources
to serve consumers. To be able to respond to customers’ needs,
accurate sales forecasting is essential to prepare appropriate lev￾els of stocks. This research aims to find methods and features to
forecast the daily sales for a case study retail store chain having
many categories of products with varied promotion prices. Three
main models are considered: (1) Time series forecasting, i.e., TBATS
model, (2) explanatory forecasting method, i.e., multiple linear re￾gression, and (3) machine learning model, i.e., XGBoost. Different
types of dependent and independent variables transformation in
Stepwise regression are also considered in order to find the most
accurate results. Since there were records of the number of Covid￾19 cases in Thailand from 2020 and there was also government’s
welfare money policy during Covid-19 crisis, this paper attempts
to find how these variables affects retail sales. Weighted absolute
percentage error (WAPE) is used to compare the accuracy among
different models.

2 LITERATURE REVIEW
For retail business, it is important to understand the behaviors of
customers. Forecasting is one of the processes to understanding
characteristics, supporting factors, negative factors, and a change of
products to learn the market. The basic models for sales forecasting
were based on lagged sales in history [3]. In the past decade, the
traditional time series techniques such as simple moving average or
Box-Jenkins ARIMA have been widely used [4], moreover, machine
learning has played a big role as a great instrument for interpreting
and demonstrating data patterns not only in the retail chain but also
in other fields such as financial, cognitive science and engineering
[5].
[6] proposed methods for predicting sales revenue using Artifi￾cial Neural Network in Grocery Retail Chain in Turkey. [7] found
that multivariate studies for SKU level forecasts outperformed the
univariates in case of perishable products like bread and bakery. [8]
and [9] considered applying machine learning models to forecast
sales and analyze the impact of properties by comparing Random
Forest (RF) with promotion forecast techniques. According to the
results, it was found that the most important variables are the pro￾motion and the day of the week of daily sales. [10] considered
external factors such as seasonal, promotion, holidays, and weather
conditions in their retail demand forecasting using machine learn￾ing. [11] forecasted the daily sales of milk teas in two different stores
with traditional time series, machine learning, and deep learning.
[12] analyzed the characteristics data with intermittent demand
and proposed machine learning models for an application in retail
apparel with plenty of predictors. The upgrade version of boosting
methods called LightGBM was represented that it had an obvious
advantage in sales forecasting and reduced training time, moreover
it can stand with exogenous factors and seasons [13] [14] .
Since the COVID-19 pandemic has caused the economy to slow
down from 2020 on, it is interesting to capture how this number
affects retail sales. There were records of the number of Covid-19
cases in Thailand from 2020 and there was also government’s wel￾fare money policy during Covid-19 crisis. Unlike previous literature,
this paper also attempts to find how these variables affects retail
sales when adding these features in our proposed models.
3 DATA
The case study company separates products into 14 main groups.
However, it is found by Pareto diagram shown in Figure 1 that 7
groups of products, i.e., milk, baby milk powder, sauce, washing
powder, disposable diaper, soft drink, and cooking oil already ac￾count for 83% of total sales. This research then focuses on the top
product with the highest sales in each category. Records of daily
sales of each product from January 1tst, 2019 to October 31th 2021
are obtained for this study. After observing sales time series, there
are 3 groups of behaviors below:
1. Products with seasonality and trend: i.e., milk, and baby
powder milk
2. Products with seasonality but no trend: i.e., sauce and wash￾ing powder.
3. Products with no seasonality and no trend: i.e., disposable
diapers, soft drinks and cooking oil.
Examples from sales time series of products in each group are
shown in Figure 2-4. Table 1 summarizes the details of sales data
for each product in each group. Weight represents how much that
product’s revenue contribution to the total revenue of the focused
products. The daily sales data were divided into 3 different groups:
a) Training set from January 1st, 2019 to April 30th, 2021 (about
80%)
b) Validation set from May 1st, 2021 to July 31th, 2021 (about
10%)
c) Test set from August 1st 2021 to October 31st, 2021 (about
10%)

4.1 Forecasting Models
To identify the accurate methods and features to forecast the daily
sales for a case study retail store chain, three main models are
considered: (1) Time series forecasting, i.e., TBATS model, (2) ex￾planatory forecasting method, i.e., multiple linear regression, and
(3) machine learning model, i.e., XGBoost. The next sub section
describes details of each model.
4.1.1 Trigonometric, Box-Cox transformation, ARIMA error, trend
multiple seasonal pattern (TBATS). TBATS forecasting method is a
time series forecasting method developed by De Livera et al. in 2011
[15]. Developed from many forecasting models such as Holt-winter,
Box-cox transformation, ARIMA, TBATS helps to find parameters
using the smooth forecast method and helps to solve unobserved
patterns of time series with the Trigonometric Function method.
The highlight of the TBATS forecasting method is the ability to
forecast time series with more than one seasonal index or more
complex (Multiple seasonal pattern). TBATS equation is as follow;
