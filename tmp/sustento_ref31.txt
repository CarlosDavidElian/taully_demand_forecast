6/3 Classical decomposition

is a relatively simple procedure and forms the basis for most 
other methods of time series decomposition. There are two 
forms of classical decomposition: an additive decomposition 
and a multiplicative decomposition. These are described below 
for a time series with seasonal period m (e.g., m = 4 for quar￾terly data, m = l 2 for monthly data, m = 7 for daily data with a
